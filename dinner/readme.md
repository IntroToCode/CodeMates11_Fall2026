# Basil Pesto Pasta

## Description

Pasta tossed in a fresh, homemade basil pesto with Parmesan, pine nuts, garlic and olive oil. It's inspired by pesto alla genovese from Genoa in the Liguria region of Italy, and it's an easy dish to make in one big batch and share family-style.

### Ingredients:
- [ ] 1 1/2 pounds pasta (trofie, fusilli, penne or spaghetti)
- [ ] 4 cups fresh basil leaves, loosely packed
- [ ] 1/2 cup pine nuts
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, peeled
- [ ] 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese, plus extra for serving
- [ ] 2/3 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- [ ] 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon salt for the pesto, plus more for the pasta water
- [ ] 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- [ ] 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved (optional, for serving)

### Steps for preparation:

1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and add about
   2 tablespoons of salt.
2. While the water heats, toast the pine nuts in a dry skillet
   over medium-low heat for 3–4 minutes, stirring often, until
   lightly golden. Let them cool.
3. Put the basil, toasted pine nuts, garlic, Parmesan, lemon juice,
   1/2 teaspoon salt, and pepper in a food processor or blender.
   Pulse until finely chopped.
4. With the machine running, slowly pour in the olive oil and blend
   until the pesto is smooth. Taste and add more salt if needed.
5. Cook the pasta according to the package directions until al dente.
   Before draining, scoop out 1 cup of the pasta water and set it aside.
6. Drain the pasta and return it to the pot, off the heat.
7. Add the pesto and 1/4 cup of the reserved pasta water. Toss until
   the pasta is evenly coated, adding more pasta water a little at a
   time if it looks dry.
8. Serve in a large bowl topped with cherry tomatoes and extra Parmesan.

### Notes:

Mix the pesto in off the heat. Hot pans can turn the basil dark and
make the cheese clump.

For a nut-free version, swap the pine nuts for sunflower seeds.
Traditional Parmesan is made with animal rennet, so use a vegetarian
hard cheese if any guests are strictly vegetarian.

To feed a bigger group, double everything and cook the pasta in two
pots. The pesto can be made a day ahead; cover it with a thin layer
of olive oil and keep it in the fridge so it stays green.

For extra protein, add grilled chicken, white beans or roasted
vegetables.
