# Tiramisu-Flavoured Overnight Oats

## Description

Coffee-flavoured overnight oats with a creamy vanilla yogurt topping and a dusting of cocoa, inspired by Italian tiramisu.

### Ingredients:
- [ ] 1/2 cup rolled oats
- [ ] 1/3 cup milk of your choice
- [ ] 1/4 cup cooled brewed coffee
- [ ] 1 tablespoon of chia seeds
- [ ] 1–2 teaspoons maple syrup for the oats
- [ ] 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract for the oats
- [ ] Pinch of salt
- [ ] 1/3 cup plain Greek yogurt
- [ ] 1 teaspoon maple syrup for the topping
- [ ] 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract for the topping
- [ ] 1 teaspoon unsweetened cocoa powder

### Steps for preparation:

1. Mix the oats, milk, cooled coffee, chia seeds,
   1–2 teaspoons maple syrup, 1/4 teaspoon vanilla,
   and salt in a jar.
2. In a separate bowl, combine the yogurt,
   1 teaspoon maple syrup, and 1/4 teaspoon vanilla.
3. Spread the yogurt mixture over the oats.
4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours.
5. Dust with cocoa powder before serving.
   Add a splash of milk to the oats if needed.

### Notes:

This is a modern breakfast adaptation inspired by the
Italian dessert tiramisu, rather than a traditional
Italian breakfast.

According to Le Beccherie, a restaurant in Treviso,
Italy, tiramisu appeared on its dessert menu in 1972.
These overnight oats adapt its flavours into a
make-ahead breakfast.

Historical source: [Le Beccherie – History of Tiramisu](https://www.lebeccherie.it/en/)
