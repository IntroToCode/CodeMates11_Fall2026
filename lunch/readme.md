# Beef Strip Tacos with Pico, Guac & Chips

## Description

Seared, lime-marinated beef strips in warm corn tortillas, topped with crisp lettuce, pico de gallo and guacamole, served with tortilla chips on the side.

| Field | Info |
|-------|------|

### Ingredients:
- [ ] 1 1/2 pounds beef strips (flank, skirt or sirloin)
- [ ] 2 tablespoons lime juice
- [ ] 2 tablespoons olive or vegetable oil, divided
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- [ ] 1 teaspoon chili powder
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
- [ ] 1 teaspoon salt
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- [ ] 12 corn tortillas
- [ ] 2 cups sliced lettuce
- [ ] 1 cup pico de gallo
- [ ] 1 cup guacamole
- [ ] 8 ounces tortilla chips

### Steps for preparation:

1. Toss the beef strips with the lime juice, 1 tablespoon oil,
   garlic, cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper.
   Marinate for 20 minutes.
2. While the beef marinates, set out the lettuce, pico de gallo,
   and guacamole in bowls, and pour the chips into a basket.
3. Heat the remaining 1 tablespoon oil in a large skillet over
   high heat. Sear the beef in batches for 2–3 minutes per batch,
   turning once, until browned at the edges.
4. Move the beef to a plate and let it rest for 3 minutes.
5. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet over medium-high heat,
   about 30 seconds per side. Keep them wrapped in a clean towel.
6. Fill each tortilla with beef, lettuce, pico de gallo, and
   guacamole. Serve with tortilla chips on the side.

### Notes:

If the beef strips are thick, slice them thinner across the
grain so they stay tender. Cooking in batches keeps the pan hot,
so the beef browns instead of steaming.

For sturdier tacos, double up the tortillas. Add a squeeze of
lime, crumbled cotija, or sliced jalapeños for extra flavour.

To make the pico de gallo yourself, combine diced tomato, onion,
cilantro, jalapeño, lime juice, and salt. For homemade guacamole,
mash ripe avocado with lime juice, salt, and a little onion and
cilantro.
